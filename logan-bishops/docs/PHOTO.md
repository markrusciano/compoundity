# Reference photo: "The Logan Bishops"

Reference image (view it with the Read tool, it is a PNG, 1152x1536 portrait):
`/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-compoundity/880bcda0-2b03-5361-99df-72fbe50dae48/scratchpad/reference.png`

Three men in Halloween costumes pose in a Logan Square (Chicago) apartment at night,
all three giving the camera the same dead-serious, slightly-tilted-head deadpan stare.
That deadpan is a big part of why the photo is funny. We do not know their names; never invent
real names. Refer to them by costume: **the King** (left), **the Friar** (middle), **the Bishop** (right).
The trio together = "the Logan Bishops".

## The King (left)
- Slim build, fair skin, short brown hair (side-parted, a bit swoopy), **brown mustache, no beard**.
- Red velvet crown with gold band and gold fleur/cross details, jewels; worn slightly tilted.
- Long **red cape** with a thick **white-with-black-spots (ermine / "dalmatian") trim** down both front edges and collar.
- Under the cape: **tan/khaki chore jacket** open over a **brown t-shirt** (small printed text), **dark indigo jeans** with rolled cuffs, **black socks** (no shoes).
- Holds a **long black scepter/cane with an ornate gold knob** in his right hand at hip height. The cane runs diagonally
  from the floor at far lower-left up to the gold knob, which rests in front of the Friar's chest. It looks like he's
  ceremonially presenting the scepter across his friend.
- Head tilted to his left (toward the Friar), chin slightly down, flat eyes, mouth closed.

Heights in the photo: the Bishop is clearly tallest; the Friar's head is slightly higher than the King's hair
(the King leans and tilts his head, so he reads a little shorter); the Friar is the broadest/stockiest.

## The Friar (middle)
- Stockiest of the three, medium height; fair skin, short brown hair, **short full beard**, **rimless rectangular glasses**.
- **Plain tan/beige mottled ankle-length robe** (shepherd / friar / nativity-extra look) with long sleeves; a **black crew-neck t-shirt** collar peeks out at the neckline.
- **Hands clasped in front at the waist**, standing very upright, chin slightly up, lips pressed, deadpan. Gray socks.

## The Bishop (right)
- **By far the tallest** (head ~10% higher than the others even before the hat), lanky, fair skin, short reddish-brown hair,
  **big ginger-brown mustache**.
- **Very tall white-and-gold bishop's mitre** with ornate gold medallion patterns and a gold border (about 1/3 of his body height on top).
- White clerical collar, **red satin cape/chasuble** draped to the knees, a **vertical gold-embroidered stole** down the center front.
- Dark gray pants underneath, **gray wool socks in navy slide sandals** (the socks-and-slides is a perfect comedic detail).
- Arms hanging at his sides inside the cape, head tilted slightly down, smoldering serious stare.

## The room (Logan Square apartment at night)
- White walls; **large black-framed windows** showing the dark-blue night street outside.
- Visible outside: **glowing McDonald's-style golden arches** with a red sign beneath, directly behind the Friar/Bishop,
  a lit billboard reading **"OVER $1 BILLION RECOVERED"** (personal-injury-lawyer ad energy), apartment buildings with lit windows, streetlights.
- A big round **white globe lamp** glowing at upper left.
- A tall **dracaena / corn plant** with long spiky leaves behind the King/Friar, lit from below by a warm lamp.
- Left: kitchen island with a **white speckled terrazzo countertop**, a half-drunk pint of beer, a soda can,
  a tissue box, a cat-print water bottle, shelves with coffee bags; a cardboard box labeled **"Peanut Day"** under the island.
- Right: a desk with a monitor, **red chairs**, a **checkered cream rug**, a pair of tan clog slippers on the rug.
- **Light blond wood floor**.
