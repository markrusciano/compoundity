# The Logan Bishops: final storyboard

**Title:** Ceremonial Bonk: The Lawsuit Arc (訴訟編)
**Format:** one seamless Vine loop, **6.00 s**, square 1:1, anime limited animation in procedural SVG (see `CONTRACT.md`). Sound is optional garnish; every joke reads with sound off.
**Spine:** Pitch 1 (vine-rhythm), trimmed. Grafts: Pitch 0 (knob glint omen, socks-to-face tilt-up, mitre outgrows the frame, flash plus shutter reset, stat-card voice) and Pitch 2 (the arches as the light source, loop counter over 1 billion, eye-slide toward a target we can see).

## Logline
On Halloween in Logan Square, the King's solemn scepter ceremony ends in a bonk on the Friar's head. The Friar's deadpan survives, but his eyes drift to the injury-lawyer billboard outside the window, and a Lawsuit Arc begins. The Bishop turns out to be an RPG healer and cures him for +999 HP. The case is dismissed and the Friar is crushed. A phone-camera flash snaps all three back into the exact deadpan of the real photo, and the loop bonks again.

## Beat notes (action, sound-off read, why it is funny)

### 1. The Ceremonial Bonk (0.00-1.40)
- **0.00-0.48** The exact photo, held. The King's small temple sweat drop is there at 0.00 and fades out 0.30-0.45.
- **0.28-0.48** A 4-point kira star pops on the gold scepter knob at about (462,470): scale 0 to 1.4 to 0, rotating 45 deg, peak at 0.38. This is the first motion of every loop, and it pulls the eye to the scepter.
- **0.48-0.95** Ceremonial raise, about 6 drawings on twos. The King closes his eyes, lifts his chin, raises his right arm toward the Friar and arcs the knob up and over until it hovers about 40 units above the Friar's hair, near (500,222). The floor tip swings up off the floor. From 0.50, pink-gold shoujo bubbles and twinkles bloom behind the trio. The Bishop's pupils (and nothing else) follow the knob.
- **0.95-1.02** The knob drops onto the Friar's crown, contact about (500,258). There is a 2-frame scepter smear (two ghost copies at 40% and 20%).
- **1.02-1.09 IMPACT FRAME** Flat red background (#C8161E), all three as flat black silhouettes (crown, raised scepter and mitre keep them readable), and white starburst lines from the contact point. **ゴン!!** slams in. All bubbles pop out at once.
- **1.09-1.40** Color returns. The Friar's head squashes (headSquash 0.86, then 1.05, settling to 1.00 by 1.22), his glasses knock 8 deg askew, a cartoon bump inflates (0 to 1.1 to 1.0 by 1.18), and 3 yellow stars start orbiting it. **His face does not change.** The King goes k_oops with the knob still on the Friar's head. The Bishop's brows lift a hair.
- **Sound-off read:** a stick, a head, and the anime bonk.
- **Why funny:** in the real photo the scepter already points across the Friar, so this is what was always going to happen. The sparkly coronation slows it down so the slapstick lands harder. The deadpan surviving the bonk is the photo's own energy.

### 2. Dead Air (1.40-2.02)
- Hard cut to a locked Friar close-up. The billboard's lower part is visible, cropped by the top edge, right above his bump. Stars orbit and slow down.
- **1.40-1.56** hold. **1.56-1.84** only his pupils slide up and right toward the billboard (gazeX 0.7, gazeY -1), 4 drawings on twos. **1.84-2.02** hold with pupils pinned.
- The King is half in frame at the left edge in K_HIDE, looking away. He is completely still (rewatch tell: he hid the scepter).
- **Why funny:** Vine dead air. Limited animation does the timing, and we can see exactly what his eyes are moving toward.

### 3. The Billboard (2.02-2.70)
- **2.02-2.10** Crash zoom up onto the billboard with radial speed lines. **2.10-2.70** It glows bright, with two quick flicker dips at 2.22 and 2.46 (brightness 1 to 0.6 to 1 over 2 frames) and a slow push.
- The Friar's bump with its orbiting stars sits at the bottom of the frame directly beneath it. The golden arches glow at the lower-right edge.
- **Why funny:** the injury-lawyer billboard really is right outside their window, right above the Friar's head. Chekhov's billboard. Nothing competes with it: it is the only text on screen.

### 4. 訴訟編 The Lawsuit Arc (2.70-3.42)
- Hard cut to an extreme close-up with a dutch tilt. The room drops away to a deep purple villain panel with radiating lines and a vignette. The King and Bishop are hidden for this shot.
- **2.70-2.78** His signature clasped hands rise to just under the beard (F_STEEPLE, 2 drawings). **2.74** The still-askew lenses go opaque white, and a star glints off the outer corner of the screen-right lens (2.74-2.92). **2.80** A tiny one-sided smirk: his first expression change in the whole Vine.
- **2.78** The arc card slams in (scale 2.4 to 1.0, outBack) and holds 0.64 s.
- **Why funny:** the bonk gets its own named anime arc. The calmest, most upright man in the photo turns glasses-glint villain using his own pose from the photo, just lifted to his chin.

### 5. The Healer Arrives (3.42-4.02)
- Hard cut to the Bishop's **gray wool socks in navy slides** on the blond floor. Bishounen bubbles and sparkles drift up. The tan clog slippers on the checkered rug sit at the right edge and the Friar's robe hem at the left.

- **3.80** Lands on his face: b_hero catchlights, a sparkle on the ginger mustache, and a twitch. The golden arches glow in the window just left of his mitre.

- **CLASS: HEALER** slides in at lower-left in a JRPG menu window.
- **Why funny:** the heroic anime feet-to-face reveal starts on socks in slides, which undercuts it before it begins, and the mitre is too tall for the camera. CLASS: HEALER turns the bishop costume into a video-game class, which sets up the heal and keeps it clearly a game joke.

### 6. +999 HP (4.02-4.86)
- **4.02-4.10** Fast pull-back to a two-shot. The Friar is on the left (steepled, opaque lenses, smirk, bump) and the Bishop on the right with the stretched mitre cut off by the top edge. The billboard and the arches sit in the window between them.

- **4.10-4.24** The Bishop's right arm comes out of the cape and extends at shoulder height toward the Friar: open hand, fingers forward, a green glow ring. This is a game "cast" pose, not a raised hand. His face stays b_smolder.

- **4.20-4.44** Green heal sparkles (round dots and soft 4-point twinkles) rain onto the bump.
- **4.38** The bump shrinks and pops in a small puff, the stars vanish, and his glasses straighten and turn clear. His deadpan eyes are back, but the smirk stays: he has not realized yet.
- **4.36-4.86** **+999 HP** rises in green from his head. **4.60** The Bishop raises one eyebrow (b_oneUp) with a mustache twitch.
- **Why funny:** +999 HP is overkill for a bump, the healing light is the golden arches, and the King is whistling at the edge of frame with the evidence sticking up behind his head.

### 7. Case Dismissed (4.86-5.46)
- **4.86-4.92** Snap zoom onto the Friar. **4.92** The red **却下** hanko stamp slams diagonally across the lower right of the frame (not over his face), with CASE DISMISSED beneath and a small shake.
- **4.94** His face falls to f_disappointed: his second and last expression change. **4.96-5.30** His steepled hands sink out of frame and his shoulders drop one notch (on twos). **5.00-5.12** Anime gloom lines fade in over the top of his head.
- **Why funny:** the twist is that he is disappointed to be healed. The stamp and his face land in the same shot, so the payoff takes one read.

### 8. Snap to the Photo (5.46-6.00)
- **5.46** Hard cut to the wide shot. All three are already in the exact photo poses: scepter back on its diagonal with the knob at the Friar's chest, no bump, clear straight glasses, normal mitre, no panel, no text.
- **5.46-5.54** A yellow phone-camera focus square (four corner brackets) snaps onto the three faces (scale 1.25 to 1.0). **5.54** A white flash at 70% peak for 2 frames, plus the shutter sound. The flash is gone by 5.66 and the focus square by 5.68.
- **5.72-5.84** The King's small temple sweat drop fades in. **5.84-6.00** The frame is identical to 0.00.
- **Sound-off read:** focus square plus flash means a photo was just taken.
- **Why funny:** the real photo was taken a split second after the case was dismissed, so it shows three men pretending nothing happened. The sweat drop is the King's only tell, and then he raises the scepter again.

## Loop seam
- **Frame 6.00 equals frame 0.00 exactly:** camera (500,500) z1.00, roll 0, shake 0. All photo poses and default faces, no bump, no stars, glasses clear at 0 deg, steeple 0, cast 0, mitreStretch 1.0, `hidden` false for all. Scene normal (no impact, no panel, no bokeh, billboardGlow 1, archesGlow 1). No text, no flash, no focus square. **The King's temple sweat drop is visible (sweat 1).**
- Identical span: 5.84 through 0.28 of the next loop (0.44 s). Characters are motionless from 5.46 to 0.48 (about 1.0 s), a deliberate Vine hold right after the loudest moment.
- Audio: dead silence from 5.56 through the seam. The first sound of each loop is the knob ting at 0.38, so there is no click.
- Meaning: on first play, 0.00 is just the photo with a nervous King. On every replay it is three men pretending the Lawsuit Arc never happened. The sweat drop shows the King remembers, and the knob glint at 0.38 says he is about to do it again. Endless bonk cycle; the loop counter ticks up on each wrap.
- Implementation: drive everything from `t = LB.clock.t` (already mod 6.0). Idle motion, if any, uses `LB.loopSin` with integer cycles. Default `breathe: 0` for the whole loop: the photo stillness is the joke.

## Guardrails
**Taste and content**
- Affectionate costume humor only. The jokes are about props, poses and footwear, never about bodies or faith. No real names: the King, the Friar, the Bishop.
- The heal is a video-game spell: a sideways cast pose, green glow, CLASS: HEALER, +999 HP and a chiptune arpeggio. No raised blessing hand, no crosses (sparkles use curved 4-point stars), no halo, no upward heavenly beam, no scripture or ritual. The only "divine" light is the fast-food arches flaring in the window.
- Arches are drawn as glowing arch shapes with no wordmark. The billboard carries only its generic phrase and no firm name. The bump is cartoon slapstick that is healed on screen.
- This is genre rhythm (bonk, dead air, crash zoom, hard cut to silence), not a recreation of any specific Vine.

**Photosensitivity and reduced motion**
- Only two full-frame flash events per loop: the impact frame at 1.02 (red, 0.07 s) and the shutter flash at 5.54 (white at 0.7, about 2 frames at peak). They are 4.52 s apart in the loop and 1.48 s apart across the seam. The glint and stamp are local, not full-frame. No color inversion.
- Under `prefers-reduced-motion`: shake x0.4, flash peak 0.35, speed-line opacity x0.5, crash and snap zooms eased over 0.16 s instead of 0.06-0.08 s.

**Performance and determinism (per CONTRACT.md)**
- One world stage. Every shot is a camera move on it; there are no separate shot groups or redrawn close-ups. The rigs need enough detail for z3.4 on faces and feet and z4.8 on the billboard.
- No stage-wide filters. Glows use pre-baked radial gradients and stacked translucent strokes; a small blur on the arches element only is allowed. Whip and crash motion uses speed lines and the scepter ghosts, not blur.
- Every value is a pure function of `t`. Star orbits, sparkles and speed lines are seeded (`LB.rng` at build, `LB.hash`/`LB.noise1` at render).
